An idea I’m exploring

Kesher

A possible professional networking website for frum professionals and business owners.

Please note: Kesher has not been built. It is at the exploring and research stage. There is nothing to sign up for yet, and I haven’t decided whether it will go ahead.

What is the idea?

Kesher would be a professional networking website for the frum community: a place to connect with other professionals and business owners, share your work and milestones, and hear about opportunities, in a setting that sits right with our community.

Where things stand

Right now I’m researching whether people would really use it and what they’d want from it. Your answers will help decide whether Kesher gets built and, if it does, what it should include.

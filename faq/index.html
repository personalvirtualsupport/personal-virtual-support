FAQ

Questions you may be asking

A few simple answers about how Personal Virtual Support works and what kind of help I offer.

What kind of tasks can you help with?

I help with behind-the-scenes business support such as email follow-up, scheduling, admin tasks, trackers and spreadsheets, file and document organisation, systems, client communication, and AI-assisted tasks.

Who is Personal Virtual Support for?

It is mainly for busy business owners, professionals, and small teams who need reliable support but do not necessarily want to hire someone full-time.

Do I need to know exactly what I need help with?

No. Many people know they feel overwhelmed, but are not sure what to hand over first. We can start with a short conversation, look at what is taking up your time, and work out where support would make the biggest difference.

Can you help with tech and online systems?

Yes. I regularly help clients with websites, email platforms, forms, automations, Google Workspace, Zoom and other online business tools. I am not a software developer, but I am very comfortable troubleshooting systems, learning unfamiliar platforms, and working out practical solutions.

Can you help with AI?

Yes. I use AI as a practical tool to work more efficiently, organise information, draft content, clean up documents, create systems, and find sensible solutions to admin and business support tasks.

Do you work hourly or as a package?

It depends on what kind of support you need. We can discuss the scope together and choose the best way to work, whether that is a clear project with a fixed price agreed in advance, regular ongoing support, or a more flexible arrangement.

How do we get started?

The first step is a short, no-pressure chat. You can tell me what is taking up your time, what feels messy or stuck, and what kind of support would feel most helpful. From there, we can decide whether it makes sense to work together.

What makes this different from using a larger agency?

Personal Virtual Support is a dedicated, one-person service. You work directly with me, so the support is personal, consistent, and focused on understanding your business properly.

Do you offer website design as well as admin support?

Yes. Alongside virtual admin support, I design and build clean, professional websites for small businesses. You can see two recent examples in the portfolio on the home page.

Can you help coaches and consultants?

Absolutely. Coaches and consultants are some of the people I help most, taking inbox, scheduling, and client follow-up off their plate so they can focus on their clients.

Do you work with businesses in the Anglo and Jewish community?

Yes. I work closely with business owners and professionals across the Anglo and Jewish community, and I understand the rhythm, calendar, and sensitivities that come with it.

Can you work across different time zones?

Yes. I work with clients in different time zones and shape the day around when you need things done, so the time difference is never a problem.

How much does a virtual assistant cost?

It depends on how much support you need, which is why I shape it around your business rather than a fixed package. The best place to start is a short, free call so we can work out what would genuinely help.

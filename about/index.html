About

Hi, I’m Soroh Dina Lowe

I run Personal Virtual Support, a virtual assistant service based in Israel, working remotely with business owners in Israel, the UK and the US. Think of me as a dedicated right hand: one person who learns how your business works and takes the admin, tech and website jobs off your list.

What I’m especially good at

I have many years of hands-on experience in admin support and operations, client communication, documents and files, spreadsheets and trackers, research and scheduling. I’m at my best taking messy information, scattered tasks or half-finished systems and turning them into something clear that works.

I’m also quick to work out unfamiliar tools, from a WordPress website to an email platform or a Google Workspace setting. I use AI as a practical tool to learn quickly, draft and organise, while the judgement and the care stay mine.

How we can work together

Everything is done remotely, and we set it up around how you already work. Support can be hourly, ongoing or a fixed-price project.

How these were built

A short note on what went into each one.

Jewish Dating Mentor for Marisa

A one-to-one dating mentoring service needs to feel personal and private before anyone will pick up the phone. The site leads with warmth and reassurance, keeps the explanation short, and puts a single clear next step in front of the visitor.

Bernard Finlay Memorial Library

Over 1,200 books catalogued and made searchable, so members of the community can find, reserve and borrow in seconds instead of asking a volunteer. The hard part was not the design, it was organising the data behind it.

Lamar Apartment Rentals

An apartment rental service in Jerusalem serving English-speaking renters looking in Ramat Eshkol and the surrounding neighbourhoods. Warm, elegant, and easy to browse on a phone, which is where most people search for a flat.

Tiv Harahit Furniture Store

A redesign for a long-established furniture store in Ramat Beit Shemesh, showing off custom and ready-made pieces properly. Built on their existing WordPress setup so nothing they already relied on had to be thrown away.

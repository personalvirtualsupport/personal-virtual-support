About

A dedicated right hand, not a faceless agency

Personal Virtual Support is a one-person virtual support service for busy business owners who are carrying too much themselves. I work behind the scenes to keep the admin side of your business calm, organised, and moving, so the details don't fall through the cracks and you can stay focused on growth, your clients, and the work only you can do.

Who is behind Personal Virtual Support?

Personal Virtual Support is run by Soroh Dina Lowe, an experienced remote business support professional with many years of hands-on experience helping businesses stay organised, responsive, and on top of the details.

My background includes admin and operations support, content and documentation work, file and document organisation, spreadsheet trackers, client communication, follow-up, research, scheduling, and project coordination. I am especially comfortable taking messy information, scattered tasks, or unfinished systems and turning them into something clear, structured, and workable.

I am also very good at figuring things out independently. I am not afraid of new systems, unfamiliar tools, or complicated tasks, and I use AI as a practical guide to learn quickly, problem-solve, draft, summarise, organise, and get things moving faster.

Experienced support Practical experience across admin, documents, files, communication, trackers, and day-to-day business support.
Independent problem-solver Comfortable working things out, learning new tools, and bringing order to unclear or messy tasks.
AI-assisted efficiency Uses AI carefully and practically to work faster while keeping human judgement, care, and accountability.

The right-hand approach

I'm not a rotating pool of assistants or a large outsourced office. I'm one consistent person who learns your business properly: how you work, what matters to you, and where things tend to slip, and becomes a reliable extra pair of hands you can hand things to and trust they'll get done.

You can work with me in whatever way suits you: hand me a set of tasks and let me run with them, give me access to your inbox or calendar to manage directly, or have regular check-ins where we work through things together. However we set it up, the goal is the same: less in your head, more in a system, and nothing forgotten.

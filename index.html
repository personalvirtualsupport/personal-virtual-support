Business automation

Bookings and invoicing, brought together

WhatsApp → Google Calendar → Google Sheets → invoice details

The problem
A business owner was receiving booking details by voice note and WhatsApp, then typing them into the calendar and again into invoices.
What I did
Built a system connecting WhatsApp, Google Calendar and Google Sheets, so booking details can be sent by voice note, confirmed in WhatsApp and recorded once.
The outcome
The system is in use. Invoice information is prepared from the bookings for the owner to review, without retyping anything.

Event registration

Registration for a one-day business event

Payment page → Make.com → shared Google Sheet

The problem
One of the event’s workshops had only 15 places, the payment page couldn’t limit them, and the organizer needed every registration in one place.
What I did
Added the workshop choice to the existing payment page and connected each registration through Make.com to a shared Google Sheet, with a running count of workshop places.
The outcome
The workshop filled all 15 places and was taken off the checkout as soon as it was full. The organizer could check every registration in one shared sheet.

Email system
